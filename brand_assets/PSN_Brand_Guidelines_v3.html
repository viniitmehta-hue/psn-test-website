01 — Brand Architecture

One network.
Two divisions.

Pressplay Sports Network is the parent brand housing two commercially distinct divisions. Both share the master logo, PSN Blue, and brand voice. Each division has its own audience, tagline, and accent color — creating clear identity without fragmenting the brand.

Pressplay Sports Network
The sports business network for India, the Middle East & beyond · Dubai, UAE
“The sports business network.”
Two divisions
Shared: Logo · PSN Blue · Gold accent · Barlow typography · Brand voice · Website URL
Division 01
Pressplay Bridge
Sports Tech Market Entry & BD
“Where sports tech meets its market.”
Sports tech startups and scaleups from the US, Europe, and Australia entering India, the Middle East, and APAC. Pressplay Bridge is their exclusive BD partner — opening doors in 90 days that take companies years to find alone.
India MENA APAC US · EU · AU founders
Division 02
PSN Sports
Sponsorships & Sports Marketing
“One brief. Every sports screen.”
Brands and agencies seeking to partner with live sports streamers globally, with a focus on India and the Middle East. PSN Sports packages cross-platform sponsorships across JioHotstar, DAZN, Shahid, FanCode, StarzPlay, and Dream11.
Brands Agencies $50k–$500k+
How the divisions reinforce each other
Shared publisher network
PSN Sports’ publisher relationships (JioHotstar, DAZN, etc.) are live buyer introductions for Pressplay Bridge startups. One commercial network serves both revenue lines simultaneously.
Bridge feeds Sports
Sports tech products onboarded via Pressplay Bridge become technology layers in PSN Sports campaigns — AI highlights, virtual branding, measurement, and data tools.
Both feed Pressplay Capital
Every startup relationship generates deal flow for Pressplay Capital, the early-stage sports tech fund. Commercial proof-of-concept before the investment thesis — uniquely de-risked.
02 — Taglines & Messaging

Three lines.
Total clarity.

Three taglines — one for the parent brand, one per division. Each is precise, ownable, and written to work at any size from a business card to a conference stage. They are never abbreviated, swapped, or merged.

Pressplay Sports Network — Parent brandMaster tagline · Use when both divisions are represented
“The sports business network.”
Positions PSN as infrastructure — a network, not a service. Works for both BD and sports marketing audiences without favouring either. Deliberately broad. The word “network” carries dual meaning: the publisher network (supply side) and the professional relationships PSN is built on (people side).
Use on: Homepage hero, email signatures, business cards, LinkedIn company banner, conference stands, any material where the full PSN brand is represented without division distinction.
Pressplay Bridge — Division 01Division tagline · Sports tech market entry
“Where sports tech meets its market.”
A meeting point, not a one-way journey. “Meets its market” implies a perfect match, not a hard sell. The “Bridge” name and this tagline work in exact harmony — we are the crossing point between innovation and the markets ready for it.
Use on: Bridge section of website, startup outreach, Bridge pitch deck covers, LinkedIn posts about market entry, Dubai soft-landing materials, BD proposals.
PSN Sports — Division 02Division tagline · Sponsorships & sports marketing
“One brief. Every sports screen.”
The entire PSN Sports value proposition in five words. “One brief” is the pain point solved. “Every sports screen” is the scale offered. Short sentences mirror the energy of match commentary. Immediately understood by any brand CMO.
Use on: PSN Sports section of website, brand outreach, media kit covers, social content, publisher materials, any context where sponsorships/media buying is the subject.
Tagline rules — non-negotiable
Always in quotes
All three taglines are set with opening and closing typographic quotation marks (“ ”). Never straight quotes. Never removed.
Never swap them
Each tagline belongs to its context. Never use the Bridge tagline in a sponsorships proposal or the PSN Sports tagline in a startup pitch.
Typography
Always Barlow Condensed 700–900, ALL CAPS in headlines. In body copy, sentence case is permitted for inline references.
Never truncate
Never shorten a tagline to fit a layout. Reduce the font size before cutting a word. The tagline is always complete.

06 — Voice & Tone

How PSN
speaks.

The master PSN voice is direct, expert, and ambitious. Pressplay Bridge calibrates this for startup founders who need speed and certainty. PSN Sports calibrates it for brand CMOs who need simplicity and scale. Both are honest, never over-promising.

Master voice — four pillars (apply to all PSN communications)
Direct
No hedging. No vague promises. We say what we will deliver, in plain language. Short sentences. Strong verbs. Zero fluff.
Expert
We reference real platforms, real markets, real deals. 20+ years of operating inside these markets. We demonstrate knowledge without needing to explain credentials.
Connected
We know people on both sides of every deal. Warm and relationship-conscious. Every deal has a human on the other side and we write like we know that.
Ambitious
India and the Middle East are two of the world’s most exciting sports markets. We own that energy. Forward-looking, bold about claims we can back up.
Division voice — Pressplay Bridge vs PSN Sports
Pressplay Bridge voice
Operator-credible
We’ve sat in JioHotstar’s commercial office. We’ve run the sales process for Brightcove India. We speak from inside the market — not from a consultant’s playbook. Name the specifics.
Speed-obsessed
Founders are time-poor. “We get you to revenue in 90 days” beats three paragraphs about market opportunity. Always anchor on timeframes and tangible outputs.
Risk-dismantling
Entering India or MENA is daunting. PSN names that fear and then defeats it — regulatory navigation, first-hire avoidance, pre-built publisher relationships.
Deal-specific
Never “explore partnerships.” Always “three qualified publisher meetings in the first 30 days.” Name deliverables. Specificity builds trust faster than any credential.
PSN Sports voice
Decisive
Brands want to be told what to do. “Here’s what a $150k India + MENA package looks like” lands better than “what are your objectives?” Lead with the recommendation.
Insider-access
Name the platforms. Reference specific inventory. The audience should feel they’re getting access to something exclusive — not a media agency deck they could get anywhere.
Simplicity-obsessed
The entire PSN Sports value prop is about collapsing complexity. Language must mirror that — short sentences, no jargon, no ad-tech acronyms. If it needs explaining, rewrite it.
Scale-aware
Two billion sports fans. Six platforms. Two markets. We don’t shrink from the scale. “Your brand, live, in front of the most passionate sports fans on the planet.”
Copy in action — Pressplay Bridge
Pressplay Bridge — outreach to a US sports tech founder
✕ Never

✓ PSN Voice

✓ PSN Voice

Copy in action — PSN Sports

✓ PSN Voice

Co-branding rules — publishers & partners
01PSN is always primary
In all PSN-produced materials, the PSN logo appears first — top-left or most prominent position — at equal or greater size than any partner or publisher logo.
02Publisher logo row
Publisher partners appear in a single equal-sized row, separated by 1px vertical rules in gray-200. Never intermix the PSN logo in the publisher row.
03Minimum separation
Maintain at least 40px (digital) or 12mm (print) clear space between the PSN logo and any third-party logo. Never adjacent without this buffer.

Never use Cobalt in PSN Sports materials or Amber in Pressplay Bridge materials. The division accent colors are exclusive identifiers. The master PSN Blue bridges both divisions.
